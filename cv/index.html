Professional summary

Full CV available as PDF.

Michael R. Dalton is a Research Economist in the Office of Employment and Unemployment Statistics at the Bureau of Labor Statistics, a position he has held since July 2013. His research interests are labor economics, data science, machine learning, and applied microeconomics.
